## 🚀 THE 4 CORE SUMMARY PILLARS (KEY DELIVERABLES)

### **1. Eradicated the 50 km "Travel Tax"**
* Replaced physical paper sign-off registers with a remote 2-factor smartphone OTP release workflow.
* Farmers authorize stock dispatches remotely from home, issuing cryptographically signed Digital Gate Pass QR codes.

### **2. 100% Digitized Warehouse Operations (WMS)**
* Digitized weighbridge intake (gross/tare/net weight), quality grading (`A`/`B`/`C`), bag count, and printable barcode generation.
* Automated server-side PDF invoice rendering (`pdf-generator.ts`) with GST breakdown and QR payment codes.

### **3. Zero-Trust Buyer Marketplace & Escrow Security**
* Connected farmers directly with institutional buyers, bypassing predatory intermediaries (*Arhtiyas*).
* Protected transactions via nodal **Escrow Accounts (`EscrowTransaction`)**, holding buyer funds until physical truck loading.

### **4. Production Engineering & Offline Synchronization**
* Delivered Express 5 APIs, PostgreSQL 16 DB, Redis 7 cache, Next.js 16 Web WMS, and Expo 56 Mobile Apps (100% test pass rate).
* Built client offline queueing (`offline-queue.ts`) with Redis idempotency headers (`X-Idempotency-Key`) for zero rural data loss.
